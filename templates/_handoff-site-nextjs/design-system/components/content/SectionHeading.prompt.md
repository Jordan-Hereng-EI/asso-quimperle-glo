Composed section header — eyebrow, big display title, optional lead.

```jsx
<SectionHeading
  eyebrow="Nos missions"
  title="Agir pour les enfants"
  scriptWord="béninois"
  lead="De Quimperlé au Bénin, nous soutenons l'éducation, la santé et l'autonomie des familles."
  align="center"
/>
```

`scriptWord` is appended in the handwritten Caveat accent. Use `ondark` on dark sections, `align="center"` for centered blocks.
